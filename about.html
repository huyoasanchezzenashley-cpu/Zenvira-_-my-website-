01 / OUR BEGINNING

✳

A little less ordinary.
A little more you.

ZENVIRA began as a creative idea: what if everyday jewelry felt like a small piece of art? Our fictional studio imagines accessories with thoughtful shapes, gentle contrasts, and a personality all their own.

Every piece in this showcase is an original concept created for this student project. The names, designs, and collection are fictional—not existing commercial products.
